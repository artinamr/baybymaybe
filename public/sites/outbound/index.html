Who we are

A small crew with serious local mileage.

OUTBOUND runs a short list of trips, a short list of days and groups of eight at most. Every route is scouted on foot each season, every departure is planned around the forecast, and every guest gets a real pack list, not a PDF of disclaimers.

We take the practical side seriously so the wild side can do its work. Days start early, walk at the group’s pace, and finish with stories you’ll be retelling for years. Prices below are illustrative for this concept. The format is the real thing: one guide, one route, no filler.

  • 01Eight, tops. Small groups, one lead guide, real conversations.
  • 02Scouted yearly. Routes re-walked every season before anyone books.
  • 03Weather-first. We call go / no-go early and honestly.
  • 04Kit sorted. Technical gear included, pack list sent in advance.

The guided experience

Four things we sweat,
so you don’t have to.

01

Planning

Routes are scouted each season, tides and daylight are checked per departure, and your pack list arrives a week out. You just show up with boots.

02

Equipment

Packrafts, paddles, dry bags and group first-aid are ours. A clear bring-list covers the personal layer, with rental options for the big items.

03

Local knowledge

We know which saddle catches the wind by 11am, which cove is calm at mid-tide, and where the good coffee is on the drive out.

04

Small groups

Six to eight guests, one guide. Enough people for a shared story, few enough that the mountains stay the main character.
