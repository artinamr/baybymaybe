Our bakery

A neighbourhood bakery, built on craft

Since opening in 2019, Butter Days has grown from a single-oven bakery into a neighbourhood favourite, supported by a dedicated team and our long-kept sourdough starter.

We laminate by hand, ferment our bread slowly and bake in small batches throughout the morning. Every choice is guided by care for our ingredients, our craft and the people we serve.
