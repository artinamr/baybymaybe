How we build it

Built in layers, finished by hand

We bake each component separately, then assemble the cake the day before, so the layers settle and the crumb stays tender.

A brown-butter sponge gives a nutty depth. Between the layers we spread Swiss buttercream (light, glossy and not too sweet), then finish with seasonal fruit and a considered detail.

Whether it is a first birthday or a forty-year anniversary, the same care goes into every cake.

Typical ingredients

  • Brown-butter sponge (wheat flour, free-range eggs)
  • Seasonal fruit, including poached Nashi pear in spring
  • Swiss buttercream, real vanilla
  • Almond meal, dark chocolate, dried flowers

Allergens

  • Usually contains: gluten, dairy, egg, nuts
  • Varies by design. We confirm ingredients with every enquiry.

Sample information for demonstration only. We confirm allergens when planning each cake.

Serving suggestions

  • Serve at room temperature for the best texture
  • Cut with a hot dry knife for clean layers
  • Keeps two days covered at room temperature
  • Serve after the speeches, before the goodbyes
