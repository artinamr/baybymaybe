Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book a wedding cake?

For peak season, November to March, we recommend enquiring four to six weeks ahead. Winter dates may have more flexibility, so please get in touch if your date is approaching.

Can you do gluten-free or dairy-free?

Some recipes can be adapted, including our flourless orange and almond cake. Our kitchen also handles wheat and other allergens, so we will discuss ingredients and preparation with you before confirming an order.

Do you deliver?

Delivery may be available in Wellington’s inner suburbs for an additional fee. Collection from our Kelburn bakery is complimentary.

Can we taste before committing?

For weddings and larger events we offer a tasting box for $25. The tasting fee is deducted from your final order.
